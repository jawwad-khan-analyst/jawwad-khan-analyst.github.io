About Me

Who I am & what I do

I'm a Data & Financial Analyst with 6+ years of experience helping organizations move from guesswork to evidence-based planning. My work sits at the intersection of finance and data science: I model revenue and costs, build forecasts, and design dashboards that executives actually use every day.

My toolkit spans Python, SQL, Power BI, Tableau and Excel — chosen pragmatically per problem, never for show. I care about clean, explainable analysis that stands up to scrutiny and drives measurable outcomes, from cost savings to faster monthly closes.

Beyond the numbers, I enjoy translating complex findings into simple visuals and stories that stakeholders at every level can act on.
